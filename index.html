About

I am an Algorithm Engineer at SAP, where I build WalkMe's next-generation computer-using agent with the R&D team in Tel Aviv and help group companies in Japan move from traditional machine learning to LLM-based systems. Before SAP, I spent three years as a Customer Engineer at Scale AI, leading proofs of concept and enterprise deployments for customers in Japan, and worked as an AI researcher at Toshiba.

My research asks how far large language models can be trusted in medicine, particularly in Japanese. I build benchmarks for safety and clinical knowledge (JMedEthicBench, JMed48k, KokushiMD-10) and LLM agents for public-health communication. My doctoral work was in Medical Informatics at Kyoto University, after electrical-engineering degrees from Harbin Institute of Technology, where I started out in computer vision.
